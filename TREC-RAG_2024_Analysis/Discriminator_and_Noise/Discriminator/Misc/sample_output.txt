Vicarious trauma is a type of indirect trauma that occurs when a person is exposed to the traumatic experiences of others, often through their work or personal relationships. This can lead to symptoms similar to those of post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), such as intrusive thoughts, avoidance of reminders of the trauma, and changes in mood and behavior.

Coping with vicarious trauma can involve a variety of strategies, including seeking support from colleagues, friends, or a mental health professional, engaging in self-care activities, and learning and practicing stress management techniques. It is also important to recognize the signs of vicarious trauma and take steps to prevent it from becoming overwhelming. This may include setting boundaries, taking breaks from work or other stressful situations, and seeking out positive experiences and relationships.
