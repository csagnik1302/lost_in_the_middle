
[61] {What is Trauma-Informed Care? | Trauma-Informed Care Implementation Resource Center}: {Trauma-informed care is a strengths-based framework that is grounded in an understanding of and responsiveness to the impact of trauma, especially when trauma has been chronic, complex, or developmental. It recognizes that trauma affects all aspects of life and health and requires a systemic and ecological response. Trauma-informed care also emphasizes physical, psychological, and emotional safety for both providers and survivors, and helps survivors rebuild a sense of control and empowerment. Trauma-informed care is not a treatment or a therapy. It is a framework that informs service delivery and organizational culture. Trauma-informed care is not a one-size-fits-all approach. It is a flexible framework that can be adapted to meet the needs of diverse populations and settings. Trauma-informed care is not a replacement for evidence-based practices. It is a way of integrating evidence-based practices into a trauma-informed framework. Trauma-informed care is not a cure for trauma. It is a way of recognizing and responding to the impact of trauma in a way that is healing and empowering for survivors.}
